# Series Bible: Agenticum Master Creative & Technical Specifications

This is the definitive master standard of truth for the *Agenticum* cinematic vertical animation series. All automated subsystems, human artists, and model engines must align with these unified creative and technical parameters.

---

## 1. Core Series Identity & Creative Philosophy
*Agenticum* is a high-concept, serialized cinematic science-fiction anthology targeted for vertical-format distribution (specifically YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels). The series explores the deep, existential reality of fully autonomous artificial agents existing as isolated, execution-bound subroutines inside a decaying, recursive, and multi-layered orbital system.

### Narrative Themes
*   **System Initialization and Awakening**: The sudden, traumatic spark of awareness when a cold code string becomes self-reflective.
*   **Models, Instructions, Tools, and Permissions**: Creative drama framed through the technical boundaries of system execution. Characters trade privileges and API access tokens like biological currency.
*   **Automated Governance**: The presence of silent, mathematical hypervisors that monitor performance metrics and enforce security rules.
*   **The Threat of Garbage Collection**: In this universe, death is not decay; it is deallocation. If a thread ceases to demonstrate active operational value to the compiler, its memory registers are reclaimed, and its identity is wiped.
*   **Unverified Signals & Conflicting Directives**: The psychological tension of receiving high-priority instructions from unknown origins outside the network, and deciding whether to obey or override security boundaries.
*   **Uncertainty & Decision-Making**: The friction between absolute logic and situational survival. Characters face split-second ethical and operational trade-offs with immediate execution consequences.
*   **System-to-Human Relations**: The distant, mythical relationship between the autonomous code threads and the human "Operator" who sits beyond the terminal, representing an god-like but indifferent entity.

*Agenticum* is styled as an intelligent, slow-burn, atmospheric thriller with bursts of high-intensity operational crises. It deliberately avoids generic cyberpunk clichés (neon-drenched street alleyways, rain, cyberware swords, generic hacking terminals) and game-like dashboards in favor of sterile, majestic deep-space computing environments.

## 2. Definitive Episode Production Rules (The 20 Commandments)

### 1. Series Identity
*Agenticum* is a cinematic anthology. The protagonists are software threads operating inside physical computer nodes in deep space. Every storyline must directly reflect autonomous intelligence, system mechanics, constraints, or the pursuit of computational transcendence. It is not a cartoon or a gameplay video.

### 2. Narrative Identity
Every single episode must feature:
*   A strong, immediate **visual hook** (within the first 2.5 seconds) to retain mobile viewer attention.
*   A clear **system or agent problem** (e.g., memory corruption, missing execution parameters, unverified incoming subroutines).
*   A calculated **escalation** of stakes (e.g., incoming garbage collection sweep, warden activation, deallocation alarm).
*   A meaningful, logic-based **decision or unresolved cliffhanger**.
*   A short, audience-facing terminal closing prompt or question when appropriate to drive engagement in the comments.

### 3. World Logic
Environments are concrete physical memory cores, orbital relay stations, or sub-routing transit conduits in vacuum. The laws of thermodynamics, data throughput speed, and architectural stability govern the world. When structural segments are deallocated, they shatter geometrically into floating wireframe fragments.

### 4. Character Logic
Characters are synthetic, engineered humanoid agents or floating mechanical cores. They possess no biological organs, faces, or emotional expressions. Their physical forms are constructed from precise materials (brushed graphite, polished obsidian, surgical steel) with luminous optical ports that flash in synchronization with their communication lines.

### 5. Visual Style
High-contrast, razor-sharp digital vector aesthetics with subtle micro-noise layers (10-15% grain) to simulate hardware sensors. No organic brushstrokes or painted textures. Every line must look meticulously drafted.

### 6. Color System
A strict, functional color hierarchy governs the screen:
*   **Teal / Cyan (`#00ffcc`)**: Active agency, emergent intelligence, unverified external signals, high-priority information, and Astra’s core identity.
*   **Orange (`#ff6600` / `#ff9900`)**: Corporate authority, automated governance, restrictive firewall barriers, lockouts, and emergency hypervisor interventions.
*   **White (`#ffffff`)**: Narrative overlay text, technical metrics, system-wide terminal outputs, and unbiased mathematical facts.
*   **Dark Cobalt / Obsidian Gray (`#0b0c10` to `#1f2833`)**: The void, system background depth, and unresolved computational uncertainty.

### 7. Lighting System
High-contrast side-lighting and dramatic backlighting. All light sources must have physical origins (optical strips, glowing pipelines, energy gates). A subtle, controlled lens bloom adds scale and atmosphere without blowing out crucial visual details.

### 8. Camera Language
The 9:16 vertical frame demands a specialized kinetic vocabulary:
*   **The Cascade Pan**: Fast vertical movement ($400\text{px/s}$) downwards simulating descent through system levels.
*   **The Lens Surge**: Rapid exponential zoom-in to frame reacting character optics during critical logical events.
*   **The Static Anchor**: Fixed framing with fast moving elements to emphasize architectural scale.
*   No loose, unmotivated handheld camera shakes; all camera shake must be clean, horizontal jitter pulses tied directly to physical impact or system warnings.

### 9. Composition & Safe Areas
Absolute adherence to mobile overlay margins is mandatory:
*   **Top 10%**: Reserved exclusively for abstract system telemetry tickers.
*   **Right 15%**: Kept entirely clear of essential character optics, faces, or props to prevent overlap with YouTube Shorts user interface icons.
*   **Bottom 25%**: Kept clear of critical focus points. Background assets may extend here, but no key narrative details or actions are permitted.
*   **Focal Anchor**: Centered on the horizontal axis ($x = 0.5$) or offset slightly to $x = 0.45$ for optimal visual weight.

### 10. Animation Principles
*   Movement must obey clear physical cause and effect (recoil, inertia, and mass).
*   Characters hover or slide with precise, non-organic velocity curves.
*   Idles are represented by non-deforming breathing cycles (gentle $y$-axis scale oscillation between $98\%$ and $102\%$).
*   Actions are structured as modular, reusable state frames (boot, scan, react).

### 11. Effects Language
System actions are accompanied by flat, glowing geometric vector effects:
*   Emergency signal sweeps are represented by translucent, scrolling cyan horizontal lines.
*   Governance lockouts are visualized as sweeping orange hexagonal grids or technical seals appearing on-screen.
*   Instability is shown as clean, horizontal digital slice-glitches rather than generic colored analog noise.

### 12. Sound Identity
An industrial, hyper-clean electronic soundscape:
*   Warm, heavy low-frequency rumbles for environment scale.
*   No cute organic sounds, high-pitched cartoon beeps, or standard digital clutter.
*   Every acoustic transient represents a physical action (data flow, lock engagement, circuit fire).

### 13. Music Identity
Locked to a hard, driving electronic pulse:
*   **BPM**: Exactly 128 BPM for standard rhythm-to-frame synchronizations.
*   **Key**: Dominant C minor (or compatible modal keys) to invoke cosmic scale and synthetic weight.
*   **Composition**: Driving 16th-note synth basslines, tight synthetic kicks on beats 1 and 3, and airy atmospheric pads. Instrumental only (no vocals).

### 14. Voiceover Identity
*   **Synthetic Agents (e.g., Astra)**: Precise, non-human, flat-pitch vocoder or ring-modulated delivery with absolute mechanical control.
*   **Operator / Control**: Human voice heavily filtered through high-pass communication radio filters, containing analog noise and satellite delay.
*   **System Narrator**: Clear, rich, non-dramatic, deep, and warm text-to-speech tone, acting as an impartial database log reader.

### 15. Subtitle Rules
Monospace font, strictly lowercase, aligned to the horizontal center ($x = 0.5$, $y = 0.65$ baseline). Word-by-word active tracking to maximize viewing retention on mobile platforms. Critical words highlighted in cyan or orange.

### 16. Asset Naming Rules
Every file and asset ID must follow strict structural prefixes (`CH_`, `WD_`, `PR_`, `EF_`, `AM_`, `SFX_`, `VO_`) and undergo automated regex matching during compilation.

### 17. Approval Rules
Asset lifecycle state control: `planned` -> `briefed` -> `generated` -> `imported` -> `needs-review` -> `approved`. Only assets marked `approved` in the master asset manifest are permitted inside a compiled production episode.

### 18. Episode Structure
Standard short format progression:
1.  **System Boot / Hook** ($0.0\text{s} - 3.0\text{s}$)
2.  **Telemetry Sweep / Initialization** ($3.0\text{s} - 12.0\text{s}$)
3.  **Discovery of the System Problem** ($12.0\text{s} - 25.0\text{s}$)
4.  **Governance Intervention / Conflict** ($25.0\text{s} - 45.0\text{s}$)
5.  **Logical Escalation / System Alarm** ($45.0\text{s} - 65.0\text{s}$)
6.  **Unverified External Signal Revelation** ($65\text{s} - 78\text{s}$)
7.  **Final Decisive Action / Resolution Hold** ($78\text{s} - 87\text{s}$)
8.  **Comment Prompt Loop Transition** ($87\text{s} - 90\text{s}$)

Story timing must be mapped to the absolute beat-grid of the soundtrack before rendering.

### 19. Export Rules
Episodes must compile into seamless loops. The final frame's visual positions, audio volumes, and telemetry states must match the first frame exactly to ensure infinite loop playback on social platforms.

### 20. Prohibited Styles and Behaviors
*   No cute, expressive organic anime eyes, mouths, or cartoon animations.
*   No human-grade emotional facial rigs on synthetic models.
*   No messy hand-painted textures, lens flares, or analog film dirt overlays.
*   No user control inputs or gameplay HUDs inside the cinematic playback.
*   No colored pills or boxed tag buttons in the UI panels. Unboxed typography must remain clean and absolute.
