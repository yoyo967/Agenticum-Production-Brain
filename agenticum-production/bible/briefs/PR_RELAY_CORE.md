The Relay Core is the primary operational objective in Episode 001. It is a highly energetic, physical data transceiver node.

*   **Form Factor**: A massive, heavy, industrial geometric cylinder constructed of dark surgical steel and tungsten casing blocks. 
*   **Interface Panels**: Outfitted with rigid gold terminal busbars (`#c5a059`) and floating linear copper wire coils wrapped around a central vacuum chamber.
*   **Luminous Core Optic**: The center of the core houses a floating crystalline vacuum tube containing a highly energetic, pulsing cyan data plasma core (`#00ffcc`).
