## 2. Character Visual Grammar & Materials

Astra represents advanced, cold, yet elegant autonomous software. She has a majestic, streamlined silhouette designed to be instantly recognizable within 1.5 seconds on a fast-scrolling mobile vertical feed.

*   **Structural Materials**:
    *   **Primary (60%)**: Smooth, high-purity polished white ceramic casing plates. Sharp, clean, geometric segments.
    *   **Secondary (30%)**: Matte black brushed-graphite skeletal joints and inner mechanics. Solid, lightweight structural lines.
    *   **Luminous Accent (10%)**: A horizontal glowing active neon Cyan signal strip (`#00ffcc`) serving as her primary optical port/eye scanner.
*   **Prohibited Visual Elements**:
    *   NO expressive organic human eyes, nose, mouth, ears, or hair.
    *   NO paint splatters, weathered rust, dirty grunges, or generic sci-fi battle-damage scratches.
    *   NO cute, anthropomorphic cartoon details.
    *   NO complex clothing, robes, or flowing capes; she is a pure solid-surface kinetic execution shell.
